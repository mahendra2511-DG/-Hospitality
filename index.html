Data Model

Two fact tables at two different grains

fact_bookings (one row per booking) and fact_aggregated_bookings (one row per property × date × room type) share three dimensions and meet on a 3-column composite key. That asymmetry is worth naming precisely in an interview.

1 · Data model and the analysis view

The full Shodwe model: fact_bookings in the centre, joined to dim_hotels, dim_date and dim_rooms, with fact_aggregated_bookings hanging below it. On the right is the SQL for vw_hotel_booking_analysis, the single view that pulls bookings, hotel, date, room and capacity together so your dashboards read one clean table.

2 · Two facts at two grains, three shared dimensions

fact_bookings has one row per booking (134,590 rows); fact_aggregated_bookings has one row per hotel × day × room (9,200 rows) and holds capacity and successful bookings. Both join to the same three dimensions, and to each other only on property_id + check_in_date + room_category together. Use all three keys or occupancy % will be wrong.

Watch out for this

Common QA mistakes on this project's KPIs

Before you sign off on any KPI card or chart, check for these

  • Revenue = SUM(revenue_realized), never revenue_generated: cancelled bookings keep only 60%.
  • Occupancy and RevPAR take capacity from fact_aggregated_bookings: summing capacity after joining to bookings repeats it on every booking row.
  • Weekend = Friday + Saturday (stakeholder rule), not Saturday + Sunday.
  • Mix % measures need ALL() in the denominator, or every row shows 100%.
  • Average Rating skips blanks: show coverage (42.1% of bookings) next to it.
  • WoW measures need the wn column, and week 1 has no prior week.
  • Reconcile every KPI against the equivalent SQL query before presenting.

Interview Trap

Key difference: ADR vs. RevPAR vs. Occupancy

Three numbers that move together but answer different questions. Mixing them up is the most common hotel-metrics mistake in interviews.

MetricFormula (this project)What it measuresShodwe value
Occupancy %Successful bookings ÷ capacityHow full the hotels are.57.9%
ADRRevenue ÷ total bookingsAverage revenue per booking (industry ADR uses room nights).$12,696
RevPARRevenue ÷ capacityRevenue per available room: price and occupancy in one number. Always ≤ ADR when occupancy < 100%.$7,347
